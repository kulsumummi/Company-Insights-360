Project Overview & Objectives

Solving core business challenges across HR workforce management and sales revenue growth

🎯 Business Objective

The primary goal of this project is to integrate departmental HR records and sales transaction logs to answer critical management questions regarding compensation efficiency, regional sales growth, and workforce distribution.

🔍 Analytics Scope

Covers 5 departments, 100 employee records, and 1,000 sales transactions closed between 2020 and 2024 across North, South, East, and West sales territories.

💡 Core Problem Solved

Identifies top revenue-generating sales representatives, evaluates department budget utilization vs salary spend, and pinpoints product categories with maximum profit margins.

Datasets Architecture

Detailed field dictionary for the 3 interconnected business tables

🏢 departments

5 Rows Master

Stores department leadership, budget allocation, and target headcount.

  • Department (PK) Unique Department Name
  • Manager Department Head
  • Budget Annual Budget (INR)
  • Headcount Target Staff Size

👤 employees

100 Rows Master

HR records detailing salaries, ratings, experience, and cities.

  • EmployeeID (PK) Unique Staff ID
  • Department (FK) Links to departments
  • Salary Annual Base Pay
  • PerformanceScore Rating (1.0 - 5.0)

🛒 sales

1,000 Rows Log

Sales transactions closed by sales reps across regions and categories.

  • OrderID (PK) Unique Transaction ID
  • EmployeeID (FK) Links to employees
  • Sales & Profit Revenue & Net Profit
  • Region & Category Territory & Product Type

MySQL Database & Queries

Structured SQL DDL schema and business analysis queries

💾 DDL Schema & Relational Integrity

Created database company_insights_db with strict InnoDB foreign key constraints (ON DELETE CASCADE) linking employees.Department → departments.Department and sales.EmployeeID → employees.EmployeeID.

  • 1. Aggregations & Grouping

    Used GROUP BY with SUM(), AVG(), and COUNT() to summarize department salaries and regional revenue.

  • 2. Filtered Grouping (HAVING)

    Used HAVING AVG(Salary) > 80000 to identify high-paying departments after aggregation.

  • 3. Conditional Bucket Logic

    Implemented CASE WHEN statements to categorize staff into High Performer, Average, and Needs Improvement tiers.

  • 4. Relational Joins

    Utilized INNER JOIN between employees and sales to rank top sales representatives by total revenue.

🧮 DAX Measures Setup (`powerbi/DAX_NOTES.md`)

  • Total Sales

    Total Sales = SUM(sales[Sales])

  • Total Profit

    Total Profit = SUM(sales[Profit])

  • Profit Margin %

    Profit Margin % = DIVIDE([Total Profit], [Total Sales], 0)

  • Total Employees

    Total Employees = COUNTROWS(employees)
